## About the Laboratory

The Philadelphia Flight Control Laboratory (PFCL) is the heart of the Guidance, Navigation, and Control (GNC) research group within the Faculty of Aerospace Engineering at the Technion – Israel Institute of Technology.

The scope of interdisciplinary research performed in the laboratory includes:

* **High-Level Control Objectives:** Cooperative team mission planning (task assignment), multi-robot coordination, and motion planning (guidance) with regard to optimizing trajectories for dynamical systems.
* **Low-Level Control & Trajectory Following:** Robust control of single vehicles and platforms, trajectory following, and flight stability.
* **Autonomous Navigation & Perception:** Vision-aided single- and multi-vehicle autonomous navigation in uncertain and unknown environments.
* **Advanced Flight Systems:** Advanced flight displays, pilot-vehicle modelling, active manipulators, and flight testbeds.

### Research Groups within PFCL

The laboratory brings together leading research groups and faculty members across aerospace control:

* **[Cooperative Networks and Controls Lab (ConNeCt)]({{ '/labs/connect/' | relative_url }}):** Led by Prof. Daniel Zelazo, focusing on analysis and design of networked and multi-agent systems using graph theory and control theory.
* **[Autonomous Navigation and Perception Lab (ANPL)]({{ '/labs/anpl/' | relative_url }}):** Led by Assoc. Prof. Vadim Indelman, investigating single and multi-robot collaborative autonomous navigation, perception, and SLAM in uncertain environments.
* **[Cooperative Autonomous Systems Lab (CASY)]({{ '/labs/casy/' | relative_url }}):** Led by Prof. Tal Shima, spanning cooperative team mission planning, motion planning, differential games, and guidance laws.
* **[Idan Group]({{ '/labs/idan-group/' | relative_url }}):** Led by Assoc. Prof. Moshe Idan, concentrating on stochastic estimation, control with non-Gaussian noises, and low-cost avionics for RPVs.
* **[Ben-Asher Group]({{ '/labs/ben-asher-group/' | relative_url }}):** Led by Prof. Yossi Ben Asher, specializing in Optimal Control Theory from the Calculus of Variations point of view.
* **[Oshman Group]({{ '/labs/oshman-group/' | relative_url }}):** Led by Prof. Emeritus Yaakov Oshman, focusing on information fusion, optimal estimation, GN&C systems, and FDI.
* **[Flight Control Teaching Laboratories]({{ '/teaching/' | relative_url }}):** Managed by Lab Engineer Ruslan Arhipov, providing hands-on experimental facilities for both Aerospace and Mechanical Engineering courses.

### Location & Facilities

The laboratory is located in the **Lady Davis Building** at the Technion Faculty of Aerospace Engineering in Haifa, Israel, housing state-of-the-art quadcopter flight test cages, robotic ground platforms, rotary inverted pendulums, DC motor control stations, and computational hardware.
