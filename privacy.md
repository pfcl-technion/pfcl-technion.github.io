**Last updated:** October 8, 2026

The Philadelphia Flight Control Laboratory ("PFCL", "we", "us", or "our") operates the PFCL Showcase Website (`pfcl-technion.github.io`). 

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our website, in accordance with the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 1981 and the Data Security Regulations, 2017.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

The PFCL website functions primarily as a static, read-only brochure. We do not actively collect personal data via native forms. Any contact initiated by you (e.g., via `mailto:` links) will be processed solely for the purpose of responding to your inquiry.

## 2. Tracking and Cookies

Currently, this website does not deploy active tracking scripts (such as Google Analytics) and does not set non-essential cookies. 

## 3. Third-Party Services and External Links

Our website may contain links to or embeds from third-party services that are not operated by us. For example, our Contact page embeds a Google Maps iframe.

Please be aware that these third-party sites and services (such as Google) may set their own third-party cookies when their content is loaded and may collect information about you in accordance with their own privacy policies. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

## 4. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. If we implement forms for data collection in the future (e.g., project inquiry forms), explicit consent mechanisms will be provided.
