## Dedication to the Philadelphia Chapter

The **Philadelphia Flight Control Laboratory (PFCL)** owes its name to the generosity and dedication of the **Philadelphia Chapter of the American Technion Society (ATS)**, whose members adopted the Laboratory in **1989**.

The Chapter’s continuous commitment and philanthropic support provided the computational infrastructure, instrumentation, and experimental hardware that enabled generations of faculty, graduate researchers, and undergraduate students to conduct pioneering research in flight control, guidance, navigation, and autonomous systems.

### Founding Leadership & Tributes

* **Prof. Shmuel Merhav (Late Founder):** Founded the Flight Control Laboratory in September 1971. A visionary in pilot-vehicle systems, aerospace sensors, and automated control whose insights laid the foundation for decades of Israeli aerospace innovation.
* **Prof. Itzhack Bar-Itzhack (Late Co-Founder):** Pioneering leader in inertial navigation, strap-down guidance, and Kalman filtering at the Technion Faculty of Aerospace Engineering.
* **Prof. Arthur Grunwald:** Leading contributor to visual flight simulation, biodynamic interference research, and author of the 50-year PFCL history retrospective.
* **Yeheskel Ben-Arie (Chief Electronic Engineer):** Instrumental in establishing the laboratory's electronic instrumentation and early computer vision infrastructure.
* **Elhanan Rabinovitch & Benni Cooper:** Master technicians whose craftsmanship built the laboratory's first simulators and flight test tables.

## Endowed Chairs & Affiliations

Faculty members within PFCL hold prestigious endowed chairs recognized internationally:

* **Prof. Tal Shima:** Holder of the *Lottie and Max Dresher Chair in Aerospace Performance and Propulsion*.
* **Prof. Emeritus Yaakov Oshman:** Holder of the *Louis and Helen Rogow Chair in Aeronautical Engineering*.

PFCL operates within the **Faculty of Aerospace Engineering** at the **Technion – Israel Institute of Technology**, Haifa, Israel.
